# Book Analysis

AI-written analysis of each of the 66 books, in ten sections: Overview, Structural Outline, Logical Flow, Historical Setting, Themes, Keywords, Theology, Canonical Placement, Practical Living, Summary.

## Open

- **AI** menu or **Tools** menu → `Book Analysis`
- Right-click a book title in the Bible and choose a section, such as `Themes` or `Theology`.

Available in English, Traditional and Simplified Chinese.

Full guide: [Book Analysis](../tools/tool_analysis.md).
