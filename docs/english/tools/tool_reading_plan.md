# Bible Reading Plan

A 365-day Bible reading plan (Old Testament, New Testament, and Psalms/Proverbs each day) with localized book names and references in English, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.

- **Open**: `Tools` → `Bible Reading Plan` (or `Reading:::<day>` in quick search)
- **Navigation**: Defaults to today's day of the year (`1–365`), with **Previous Day**, **Next Day**, **Today**, **Next Unread**, and a **365-Day Overview** grid.
- **Reading**:
  - Click **Read** on any passage to open it in the Bible Area.
  - Click **Read All in Verses** to load all of the day's passages together in the **Verses** reader.
- **Progress Tracking**: Check **Mark Day Complete** to record progress (`reading_plan_completed` in user storage), view your completion percentage and daily **Streak**, or **Reset Progress**.
