# Getting Started

Open BibleMate AI Web in your browser. No login is needed; your settings are saved in your browser.

## The screen

- **Navigation bar (top):** avatar/title, [quick search](quick_search.md) box, and five menu buttons – Bibles, Parallel Bibles, Tools, Search, AI – followed by the Settings (gear) button.
- **Bible Area:** where you read a Bible. It has tabs.
- **Tool Area:** where parallel Bibles, tools, search results and AI features appear. It also has tabs.
- **Floating buttons (bottom right):** Swap Layout and Go (select verse).

## First steps

1. Click the **Bibles** button and choose a Bible, or type `John 3:16` in the quick search box and press Enter.
2. Click the **Go** button to jump to another book, chapter and verse.
3. Open a study tool from **Tools**, for example Cross-references, to see it next to the Bible.
4. Use the **Swap Layout** button to focus on the Bible, the tools, or both.

On a phone, tap the hamburger icon to open the drawer menu ([Mobile Layout](../layout/mobile_layout.md)).
