# Open & Navigate a Bible

## Open a Bible

- **Bibles menu** (book-shelf icon) → pick a version. It opens in the active Bible tab. See [Bibles Menu](menu_bibles.md).
- **Quick search:** type a version name such as `KJV`, or a reference such as `John 3:16`.
- Your Primary and Secondary Bibles are listed first in the menu; change them in [Preferences](../user_preferences/primary_bible.md).

## Go to a book, chapter and verse

### Go button
1. Click the round **Go** button (book icon) at the bottom right.
2. Choose a **book** (66 buttons), then a **chapter**, then a **verse**.
3. The Bible Area jumps to that verse.

### Quick search
Type a reference, e.g. `Rom 8:28`, and press Enter.

### Scrolling
Scroll within the Bible Area to read on. Each Bible tab remembers its place.

## Right-click for more

Right-click (long-press on touch screens) a verse, chapter title or book title for actions such as cross-references, commentary, copy, and notes. See [Context Menus](../layout/context_menus.md).

## Several Bibles at once

- Add tabs to the Bible Area ([Tabs Management](../layout/tabs_management.md)).
- Open a second version beside it with [Parallel Bibles](menu_parallel_bibles.md).
- Keep both on the same chapter with [Sync](toggle_options.md#sync).

## Original-language Bibles

The Bibles menu also includes Original Reader's, Interlinear, Parallel, Discourse and Linguistic Bibles (ORB, OIB, OPB, ODB, OLB).
