# Parallel Bibles

Read two Bibles side by side: one in the Bible Area, another in the Tool Area.

## Open a parallel Bible

1. Click the **Parallel Bibles** button (`devices_fold` icon).
2. Choose a Bible: Primary, Secondary, one of the original-language Bibles (ORB, OIB, OPB, ODB, OLB), or any other version.

The Bible opens in the active Tool Area tab. If the layout was Bible Only, it switches to Bible & Tool ([Toggle Layout](toggle_layout.md)).

## Menu items

`Add Parallel Tab`, `Close Parallel Tab`, `Close Others` – see [Tabs Management](tabs_management.md).

## Quick search

Type `bible:::John 3:16` to open that chapter in the Tool Area with your default tool Bible, or `bible:::KJV:::John 3:16` to specify the version.

## Synchronisation

With [Sync](sync.md) on (default), navigating in one Bible moves the other to the same book and chapter. Turn Sync off to read different passages in each window.

When viewing tagged translations or original-language Bibles side by side (for example, two tagged translations, a tagged translation alongside `OIB`/`OPB`, or `OPB` and `OLB`), hovering over a tagged Hebrew, Greek, or translated word triggers cross-pane **mutual word & clause highlighting**—highlighting corresponding words across both panes—and lets you **look up Hebrew/Greek lexicons directly** via the tooltip. See [Word Tooltips & Lexicon Links](../integrations/word_tooltips.md#mutual-word--clause-highlighting).
