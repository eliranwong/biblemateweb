# Sharing & Deep Links

- **URL:** every Bible and tool state is in the address bar ([URL Parameters](../layout/url_parameters.md)).
- **QR code:** from the avatar button ([guide](../basics/share_link_qr_code.md)).
- **Export:** AI and tool outputs download as TXT or DOCX (DOCX needs pandoc) – see [export](../ai_features/export_edit_responses.md).
- **Copy:** verse and chapter copy items in the [verse](verse_menu.md) and [chapter](chapter_menu.md) menus.
