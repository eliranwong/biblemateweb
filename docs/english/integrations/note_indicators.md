# Note Indicators

When Google Drive notes are configured and you are logged in, verses that have a note show a 📝 icon after the verse number. Click it to open the note in the Tool Area.

- Toggle indicators with `Notes` in the [Settings menu](../layout/settings_menu.md) (`notes` setting; the page reloads).
- The verse popup shows `Edit Note` instead of `Add Note` when a note exists.

See [Google Drive Notes](google_drive_notes.md).
