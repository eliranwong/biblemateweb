# Integrations

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Integration of resources for Bible study is one of the most prominent strengths of BibleMate AI Web.**
> Bibles, original-language texts, lexicons, morphology, commentaries, cross-references, treasury, dictionaries, encyclopedias, maps, timelines, indexes, notes, audio and AI are not isolated modules. They are woven together: from any verse, chapter or book you are one click from every relevant resource, opening side by side with the text you are reading and synchronised to the same passage.

## Why it matters

- **One passage, many resources.** Click a verse number and open cross-references, Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, commentaries, morphology, indexes, audio, comparison or AI in the Tool Area.
- **Hover and click on words.** Words in original-language texts and Bible notes link to lexicon and morphology data.
- **Linked everywhere.** References inside commentaries, notes and search results open in the Bible Area.
- **Synchronised.** [Sync](../layout/sync.md) keeps Bible and tool windows on the same chapter.
- **AI + data together.** AI features can call the same Bible data tools ([AI Tool Selection](../ai_features/chat_tool_selection.md)).
- **Open to other software.** A REST API, command-line client, Google Drive notes and your own data are supported.
