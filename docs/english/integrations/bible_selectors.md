# Bible Selectors

Every Bible window starts with a row of dropdowns, followed by the [three-dot menu](three_dot_menu.md).

| Selector | Function |
|---|---|
| Version | Switch to another Bible version; the passage is kept |
| Book | Choose a book (names follow your interface language) |
| Chapter | Choose a chapter |
| Verse | Choose a verse; scrolls to and highlights it |

Choosing any item reloads the passage in that window. With [Sync](../layout/sync.md) on, the other window follows.

The selectors define the context for the three-dot menu: Book/Chapter/Verse tools and searches use the selected values.

Bottom of the chapter: `◀️ Prev Chapter` and `Next Chapter ▶️` buttons.
